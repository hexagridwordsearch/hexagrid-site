Hexa Grid

Word search, on a honeycomb

A quiet puzzle game for iPad and iPhone

Hexa Grid is a word-search game played on a hexagonal grid. Words bend and curve in six directions instead of the usual eight straight lines, so finding one feels less like scanning and more like tracing a path.

110 puzzles across 11 badge sets. The first 20 are free. Unlock the rest once — no subscription, ever.

Play offline. No account, no advertising, and nothing collected about you.

Made to be calm

  • Three colour palettes, light and dark, and three text sizes.
  • Every sound can be turned off, one kind at a time.
  • Pinch to zoom and pan on a big grid; hints when a word won't come.
