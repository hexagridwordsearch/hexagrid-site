Common questions

I bought the full game, but it is locked on my other device.

Open Settings and tap Restore purchases under Purchases. A purchase is tied to your Apple Account, so use the same account on both devices.

How do I play a puzzle again?

Open the puzzle and tap the reset button in its top bar, or open a badge's page and reset all ten of its puzzles at once. Badges you have earned stay earned.

Can I move my progress to a new device?

Not yet. Progress is currently stored only on the device you play on.

How do I see the tour again?

Settings → Help → Replay tour.

A word looks correct but will not solve.

Every letter of the word must be selected, and only those letters. If you believe a puzzle is wrong, email me the puzzle's name and the word, and I will check it.
