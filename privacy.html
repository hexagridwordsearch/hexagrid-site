Hexa Grid

Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 September 2026

Hexa Grid is a word-search puzzle game. It does not collect personal information about you.

What stays on your device

Your puzzle progress and settings — which puzzles you have solved, the words you have found, the badges you have earned, and your chosen appearance, sounds and text size — are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us, and we cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

Hexa Grid offers a one-time purchase to unlock the full set of puzzles. Payment is handled by Apple. We never see or receive your payment details.

To confirm a purchase belongs to you and to restore it on your other devices, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase-management service. RevenueCat receives the purchase receipt Apple issues, a randomly generated identifier for the installation, and basic device information such as the device model and operating system version. It does not receive your name, your email address, or your Apple Account details. RevenueCat's privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

If you never buy or restore a purchase, the app does not contact RevenueCat at all.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising, and no ad networks.
  • No analytics, and no tracking of any kind.
  • No account to create, and no sign-in.
  • No access to contacts, photos, or location.
  • Nothing shared with or sold to anyone.

Children

Hexa Grid is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version handles data differently — for example by syncing your progress through your own iCloud account — this policy will be updated before that version is released.
